# Toolkit

Three small add-ons that the [products](/products) are built on. They ship in
the bundle and are documented because they're useful on their own — you can
include them in shaders that have nothing to do with terrain or water.

<Cards>
  <Cards.Card title="Eccentric Shading" href="/toolkit/eccentric-shading" arrow>
    Godot's lighting matched exactly, then extended with subsurface, light wrap
    and sheen.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Billboarding" href="/toolkit/billboarding" arrow>
    Camera-facing geometry, with four ways to keep the illusion from above.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Texture Tools" href="/toolkit/texture-tools" arrow>
    Texture arrays built in the editor, with format, size and mipmap
    reconciliation.
  </Cards.Card>
</Cards>
