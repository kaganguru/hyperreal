# Camera

Three pieces that turn a `Camera3D` into a camera: the optics in front of the
sensor, the hand holding it, and the operator walking it around.

## Lens

`Lens` folds four optical effects into one post-processing effect — chromatic
aberration, lens flare, lens dirt, and vignette with grain — and it runs on the
**Mobile** renderer, which is where most Godot projects that care about
performance actually live.

Each effect is an independent toggle. Turning one off costs nothing at all, so
you only pay for the look you're using.

<Callout type="info">
  Godot's own glow and post effects don't cover any of this on Mobile. `Lens`
  is a drop-in on either renderer.
</Callout>

## CameraRig

`CameraRig` applies layered shake to every camera under it, in the editor and
at runtime, so you can see the movement while you're framing rather than
guessing at it. Shakes stack, fade in and out, and resolve without snapping —
hold a low idle sway, add a hit reaction on top, and let it settle.

## CameraMan

`CameraMan` pilots a rig around the world, on foot or as a drone, with
smoothing on both movement and look. The result is a camera you can hand to
someone to shoot your scene with, instead of a debug fly-cam you have to
apologise for in the trailer.
