# Lens User Manual

`Lens` is a single post-processing effect containing four optical effects. Each
is an independent toggle, and a disabled effect costs nothing.

## Chromatic aberration

Channel separation that grows toward the edges of the frame, with a
**strength** control and a **radial** mode. The cheapest way to stop a render
looking digitally clean.

## Lens flare

Ghosts and halo, tuned against real camera reference rather than a stock bloom.

- **Nine ghosts**, each with its own colour and position along the flare axis.
- **A halo** with its own radius, colour fringing and intensity.
- **Source and softness controls** — what counts as a light source and how soft
  the result is, so a flare responds to your lighting instead of blowing out on
  anything bright.

## Lens dirt

Your own grime texture, lit only by genuinely bright parts of the scene — so
the smear across the lens flares when you look at the sun and stays invisible
otherwise. **Intensity** and **spread** controls.

## Vignette and grain

| Effect | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Vignette | Strength, size, softness |
| Grain | Amount, colour noise, boil rate |

The boil rate gives you grain that moves at a filmic pace instead of buzzing
every frame.
